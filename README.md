Instead of a generic greeting, DreamFire asks 5 cinematic questions about your perception, soul frequency, and creative will, then algorithmically aligns you with one of **8 Parallel Gate Dimensions**:

3. ⚡ **Zero-Day Cyber Metropolis** (Sci-Fi & Security) — Impenetrable cryptographic shields and fiber-optic skyways.
4. 🌊 **Bioluminescent Hadal Rift** (Deep Nature) — 10,000 meters beneath oceanic silence where alien coral gardens glow.
5. 🌿 **Ancient Canopy of Yggdrasil** (Living Earth) — Golden firefly swarms and blooming flora under starlight.
6. 🏛️ **Golden Pyramids of Giza** (Ancient Wonders) — Sandstone monuments aligned with Orion under the eternal desert sun.
7. 🌸 **Cloud Mountain Monastery** (Spiritual Peace) — Falling cherry blossoms and Tibetan singing bowls above morning mist.
8. 🏙️ **Midnight Rain Crossing** (Cyberpunk Tokyo) — Shinjuku neon reflections, lo-fi beats, and holographic koi fish.
